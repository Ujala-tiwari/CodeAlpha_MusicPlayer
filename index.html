ABOUT THE PROJECT

More Than Just Play & Pause

SoundWave is a responsive JavaScript music player designed with a modern interface and interactive controls. It includes playlist navigation, shuffle, repeat, volume adjustment, progress seeking, autoplay, and responsive design for a seamless listening experience across devices.
